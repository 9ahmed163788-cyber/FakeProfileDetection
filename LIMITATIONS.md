# Research Limitations and Future Directions

## 1. Research Scope

This project focuses on image-based fake social media profile classification using deep learning and image normalization techniques.

The classification task considers four categories: Bot, Cyborg, Real, and Verified.

The findings are limited to the dataset and experimental configurations investigated in this research.

## 2. Image-Based Classification

The proposed framework analyzes profile images rather than complete social media accounts.

Consequently, the approach does not directly evaluate:

- User activity and behavioral patterns.
- Posting frequency and interaction history.
- Textual content and linguistic characteristics.
- Social-network relationships and interaction graphs.
- Real-time account activity.

Image-based classification alone cannot establish whether a complete social media account is authentic or fraudulent.

## 3. Dataset Dependency

The experimental findings depend on the selected X Fake Profile Detection dataset.

Performance on other datasets, platforms, image distributions, or real-world conditions may differ.

Further evaluation using independent datasets would help assess generalization and robustness.

## 4. Experimental Configurations

The reported results correspond to the specific model configurations, normalization techniques, and optimization experiments documented in the Master's thesis.

Comparisons should therefore be interpreted in the context of their respective experimental settings rather than as universal performance guarantees.

## 5. Future Research Directions

Several directions could extend this research:

- Multimodal fake-profile detection combining images and textual information.
- Integration of behavioral and social-network features.
- Evaluation on additional and independent datasets.
- Robustness assessment under different image conditions.
- Explainable AI techniques for interpreting classification decisions.
- Exploration of NLP and language-model-based approaches to online safety and misinformation detection.

## 6. Conclusion

This project provides an experimental investigation of image normalization and independent metaheuristic optimization approaches for ResNet-50-based profile-image classification.

Its limitations also identify opportunities for extending image-based analysis toward more comprehensive and multimodal approaches to online safety.
